1. PROJECT GUIDE DEEP ANALYSIS & NOVELTY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Core Problem: Ed-tech platforms fail in rural areas due to continuous internet dependency.
- Novelty: Offline-first on-device AI architecture with intelligent sync-when-available cloud backup.
- Key Milestones:
  * Week 1: Literature review, on-device AI runtime exploration, Class 6-8 Math scope.
  * Week 2: Core app skeleton and SQLite database schema.
  * Week 3: 60-100+ NCTB curriculum-aligned question bank + quiz UI.
  * Week 4: On-device AI model integration for offline explanations/hints.
  * Week 5: Rule-based adaptive difficulty logic (3-correct up / 2-wrong down).
  * Week 6: Sync-when-available pipeline via connectivity_plus and Firebase.
  * Week 7: Usability testing with 3-5 users & performance optimization.
  * Week 8: End-to-end testing, architecture diagrams, final report.
